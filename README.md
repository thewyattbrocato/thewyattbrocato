## Try it on your own work

Both tools run in your browser, your answers stay there, and neither asks for an email to show your result.

- **[5-Task Swap][task-swap]:** four questions about one recurring task, then a plain verdict and a 15-minute test. About three minutes.
- **[AI Work Style quiz][workstyle]:** six questions that name your work style and your bottleneck.

## Built along the way

- **[design-and-copy-skills][design-and-copy-skills]:** 38 design and copywriting skills for AI coding agents. Each was tested against the same agent without it, and the losses and marginal results are disclosed.
- **[AI Deal Finder][deal-finder-live]:** search 390 product pages read in early October 2026. Each result shows the shelf price, when it was checked, and a coupon only when that product's own page printed one. The [source][deal-finder] is public.
- **[csm-kit][csm-kit]:** an offline Node.js CLI that turns CRM, ticket, and usage exports into renewal, handoff, and QBR briefs. Every factual line cites its source row.
- **[Scam First Aid][scam-first-aid]:** free, private help after a scam, with ordered steps and exact words to say. Nothing typed leaves the browser.
- **[Leverage Library][leverage-library]:** a sourced, cross-book idea library. Each idea is attributed, paraphrased, and flagged for confidence.
